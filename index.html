Suraj Gurung

Applied Economist — Causal Inference · Experimentation · Machine Learning

I'm an applied economist with a Ph.D. in Applied Economics (Food and Resource Economics, minor in Statistics) from the University of Florida. I use causal inference, experimental design, econometrics, and machine learning to understand decision-making and translate complex data into actionable insights — from food safety policy and consumer behavior to marketing and financial markets.

I'm currently an Assistant Professor of Agribusiness Management at the State University of New York at Cobleskill, where I teach agricultural economics and lead research on consumer decision-making and market behavior.
