How I work

A three-step research process.

Each project moves from framing the decision to building evidence to making the result usable.

01

Frame the decision

Translate an ambiguous business or policy question into a measurable outcome, a credible comparison, and an actionable decision rule.

Measurement strategyPower analysis
02

Build credible evidence

Use experiments, quasi-experiments, and econometric models to separate causal signal from correlation and noise.

A/B testingDiD · IV · synthetic control
03

Make results usable

Communicate uncertainty, trade-offs, and expected impact clearly so research moves from a finding to a decision.

Decision supportStakeholder communication
