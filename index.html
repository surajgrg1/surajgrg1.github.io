Marketing Analytics · Bayesian Modeling

Portfolio Project

Marketing Mix Modeling & Budget Optimization

A modeled analytics portfolio project — not a real client engagement. I did not personally manage or control this media budget; the figures below are model outputs from a self-directed analysis.

Analytical question: how should marketing investment be allocated across channels based on estimated incremental contribution? Built a Bayesian marketing mix model (Google Meridian) modeling approximately $220M in media spend.

Methods
Bayesian Marketing Mix Modeling · Google Meridian · MCMC · Channel Contribution · Incrementality · Budget Allocation · Scenario Analysis
Result
Modeled reallocation strategy lifted ROI from 0.7 to 0.8 (+$4M incremental revenue potential), with Channel 2 emerging as the most efficient channel (ROI 2.02).
